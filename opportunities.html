Connect a permitted HTTPS JSON feed you operate or an authorized data-provider endpoint. This site cannot access private APIs or scrape eBay/TCGplayer itself. Feed format: array of objects with card, set, number, ask, url, source, condition, shipping, observedAt; or an object with a listings array. An exact card identity is required.
